<objective>
Build the three-tier responsive navigation that does not exist today. Below 1000px the sidebar merely hides (D-41) and nothing replaces it — a mobile dead-end. This plan adds: a `<768px` 4-slot bottom-nav (Dashboard / Vulnerabilities / Tickets / More) with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` padding; a `768–999px` topbar hamburger that opens a slide-in drawer with all 9 destinations; and a "More" vaul bottom sheet listing the 6 secondary destinations. The `>=1000px` sidebar (D-41) is untouched.

Purpose: Satisfies UX-07-02 (4-slot bottom-nav + safe-area) and removes the UX-07-01 mobile-nav dead-end. Without it the viewport sweep would pass but the app would be unusable on a phone.

Output: `nav-items.ts` source-of-truth, `bottom-nav.tsx`, `nav-drawer.tsx`, `nav-more-sheet.tsx`, and wiring in `app-shell.tsx` + `topbar.tsx`.
</objective>
