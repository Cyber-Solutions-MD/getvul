      accessible name (the Vulnerabilities destination). Use a role/text locator, e.g.
      `page.locator('nav[aria-label="Primary navigation"]').getByRole('link', { name: /vulnerab/i })`.

      trivially reachable, e.g. the list/board `?view=` toggle, prefer clicking it; otherwise the
