|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-36-esc-ssrf | Tampering / Information Disclosure | outbound webhook POST | mitigate (HIGH) | _validate_webhook_url: https-only scheme allowlist, block private/loopback/link-local/metadata IPs+hosts, follow_redirects=False (Pitfall 10) |

| T-36-esc-tenant | Information Disclosure | cross-tenant escalation rows | mitigate | tenant_id FK + index on sla_escalation_events; every query tenant-scoped |
