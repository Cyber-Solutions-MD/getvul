    5. Open a card (single tap, no hold) — the DrillPanel should open. While a card is being dragged, confirm no layout shift and that Esc/clickaway closing the panel behaves cleanly (Pitfall 3 / D-CARD-02).
    6. Optional: toggle List/Board — the view toggle must persist in the URL.
