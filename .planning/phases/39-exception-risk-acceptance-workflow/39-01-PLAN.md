<objective>
TRACER SLICE — the thinnest production-quality end-to-end exception: an analyst grants
one FINDING-scoped exception, the finding drops out of the active vuln list, and it
resurfaces automatically on expiry OR early revoke — every mutation audited. This proves
D-01 (compute-on-read source of truth), D-04/EXC-04 (free auto-resurface), EXC-03 (audit)
before any horizontal expansion (scope semantics, SLA subtraction, full consumer sweep,
dashboards, UI) is attempted.

Purpose: de-risk the exclusion model (D-01/D-05, both rated `costly`) at minimum blast
radius. If the compute-on-read join is wrong, we learn it here against one consumer, not
after wiring 20.
Output: new `backend/app/exceptions/` module, the `exceptions` table + migration, three
endpoints, the shared `active_exception_subquery` seam, the Pattern 4 lazy-on-read
expiry-audit sweep, one wired consumer (vuln list), and a green tracer test.
</objective>

single mechanism (status flip to SUPPRESSED/FALSE_POSITIVE) to TWO parallel mechanisms
(status flip OR an active exception row). Decision: **add-alongside, do NOT promote/consolidate.**
This is D-02's explicit locked choice — the legacy `/cve/ignore`, `/remediations/suppress`,
and asset `is_ignored` paths stay untouched; exclusion is the union. Recorded as accepted
debt; the future-consolidation trigger is already logged in CONTEXT.md "Deferred Ideas"
(legacy suppress consolidation → its own future phase).
</assumption_delta_decision>
